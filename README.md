# Teach You a Lesson

## A. Introduction
Teach You a Lesson is an intense South Korean television drama series that addresses the harsh realities of school violence and youth rebellion. Set within a high-stakes academic environment, the series highlights the conflict between troubled students and determined educators trying to restore order. It weaves together elements of suspense, moral dilemmas, and intense emotional drama that keep viewers on the edge of their seats.

- **Genre:** Drama, Action, School, Youth
- **Release Year:** 2026

---

## B. Synopsis
The story unfolds in a high school plagued by severe bullying, delinquent behavior, and a broken hierarchy that the school administration continuously fails to control. When a specialized instructor with an unconventional past is brought in to restore order, the fragile power dynamic among the students quickly begins to fall apart. As underground student groups are uncovered and long-buried secrets surface, both students and teachers are forced to face the harsh consequences of their actions. The new instructor uses radical and fearless methods to challenge the bullies, proving that actions have real-world repercussions. Through thrilling showdowns and emotional revelations, the series depicts a raw journey toward accountability, personal growth, and justice.

> *"Respect is not something given freely; sometimes, it has to be taught."*

## C. Characters
### Main Cast
1. **The Lead Instructor:** A fearless and strategic educator who uses unconventional methods to discipline unruly students and enforce order.
2. **The Troubled Ringleader:** A charismatic student who controls the school's social hierarchy and openly challenges authority.
3. **The Victim-Turned-Ally:** A quiet student who seeks justice against school bullies and assists the lead instructor in uncovering corruption.
4. **The Idealistic Teacher:** A compassionate educator who firmly believes in second chances, though she struggles with the harsh realities of the system.
5. **The School Director:** A manipulative administrator who prioritizes the institution's public reputation over the safety and welfare of the students.
